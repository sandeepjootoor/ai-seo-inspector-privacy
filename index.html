AI SEO Inspector — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

AI SEO Inspector is a Chrome extension published by AI SEO Solutions. It checks the page you have open in Chrome.

Checks on your device

The extension reads the open page in your browser. It shows the title, meta description, canonical, headings, images, JSON-LD schema, social tags, same-site link status codes, which AI crawlers robots.txt allows, and whether llms.txt is present. These checks run on your device. Page content is not sent to AI SEO Solutions or any other server we operate.

Requests to the site you are viewing

When you open the side panel or refresh, the extension may request robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt, llms-full.txt, and status codes for a limited number of links on the same site. Those requests go from your browser to that website. They are not sent to us.

What this extension does not do

It does not ask you to sign in. It does not use cookies, analytics, or remote storage. It does not sell or transfer user data, and it does not use data for advertising, credit, or lending.

Copy and export

Copy and Export run only when you choose them. The report stays on your device as text, CSV, XLSX, or PDF. It is not uploaded.
